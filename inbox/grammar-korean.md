---

Q: N-이에요/예요
A: “To be”; identifies or equates a noun with something.

---

Q: N-이/가 아니에요
A: “To not be”; negates identity.

---

Q: N-은/는
A: Topic particle; marks the topic of a sentence.

---

Q: N-이/가
A: Subject particle; marks the grammatical subject.

---

Q: 있어요 / 없어요
A: Expresses existence, possession, or absence.

---

Q: V-(아/어) 주세요
A: Polite request asking someone to do something.

---

Q: V-고 싶어요
A: Expresses wanting to do an action.

---

Q: V-고 싶어요?
A: Asks what someone wants to do.

---

Q: V-아/어/여요
A: Basic present-tense polite verb ending.

---

Q: V-았/었/였어요
A: Basic past-tense polite verb ending.

---

Q: N-에
A: Marks a destination, time, or static location.

---

Q: N-에서
A: Marks the place where an action occurs.

---

Q: V-안
A: Short-form negation placed before a verb.

---

Q: V-지 않다
A: Long-form negation meaning “do not.”

---

Q: V-하다
A: Basic “to do” verb used to form many action verbs.

---

Q: N-에서/부터
A: Indicates the starting point in place or time.

---

Q: N-까지
A: Indicates an ending point or limit.

---

Q: V-고
A: Connects actions or clauses; “and” or “then.”

---

Q: N-(이)랑
A: Connects nouns meaning “and” or “with.”

---

Q: N-하고
A: Connects nouns meaning “and” or “with.”

---

Q: N-을/를
A: Object-marking particle.

---

Q: N-한테
A: Indicates the recipient of an action (“to someone”).

---

Q: N-한테서
A: Indicates the source of an action (“from someone”).

---

Q: V-고 있어요
A: Present progressive; “be doing.”

---

Q: N-개, 명, etc.
A: Counters used when counting objects, people, and other items.

---

Q: A/V-지만
A: Expresses contrast; “but” or “although.”

---

Q: A/V-는데
A: Gives background or contrast, or sets up the next statement.

---

Q: N-인데
A: Gives background or contrast after a noun.

---

Q: V-(으)ㄹ 거예요
A: Indicates future plans, intentions, or predictions.

---

Q: N-도
A: Means “also,” “too,” or “even.”

---

Q: N-만
A: Means “only.”

---

Q: V-(으)ㄹ 수 있다
A: Expresses ability or possibility; “can.”

---

Q: V-(으)ㄹ 수 없다
A: Expresses inability or impossibility; “cannot.”

---

Q: V-잘하다
A: Means to be good at doing something.

---

Q: V-못하다
A: Means to be bad at or unable to do something.

---

Q: V-는 것
A: Turns a verb into a noun (“the act of doing…”).

---

Q: V-아/어야 되다
A: Expresses obligation; “have to” or “must.”

---

Q: V-아/어야 하다
A: Expresses obligation; “have to” or “must.”

---

Q: N-보다 더
A: Comparative expression meaning “more than.”

---

Q: A-좋다
A: Means “to be good.”

---

Q: V-좋아하다
A: Means “to like.”

---

Q: V-(으)면
A: Expresses a condition; “if” or “when.”

---

Q: V-(으)ㄹ까요?
A: Asks for a suggestion or opinion, or expresses wondering.

---

Q: N-쯤
A: Indicates approximation; “around” or “about.”

---

Q: N-정도
A: Indicates degree or approximation.

---

Q: N-약
A: Means “approximately” or “about.”

---

Q: V-(으)ㄹ래요
A: Expresses the speaker’s intention or asks someone’s preference.

---

Q: V-(으)ㄹ래요?
A: Asks about someone else’s will or intention; often “Do you want to…?”

---

Q: V-(으)ㄹ게요
A: Expresses a decision or promise made at the moment of speaking.

---

Q: V-아/어/여서
A: Connects clauses meaning “because” or “and then.”

---

Q: A-같다
A: Means “seem” or “look like.”

---

Q: V-(으)ㄴ 것 같다
A: Indicates that something seems to be true (past or descriptive).

---

Q: V-는 것 같다
A: Indicates that something seems to be happening (present).

---

Q: V-(으)ㄹ 것 같다
A: Indicates that something seems likely to happen (future).

---

Q: V-기 전에
A: Means “before doing.”

---

Q: V-ㅂ/습니다
A: Formal polite speech style.

---

Q: A-(으)ㄴ + N
A: Turns an adjective into a noun modifier.

---

Q: V-는 + N
A: Turns a present-tense verb into a noun modifier.

---

Q: V-아/어요 (청유형)
A: Inclusive suggestion; “Let’s…”

---

Q: V-기 위해(서)
A: Means “in order to.”

---

Q: N-밖에 + 부정
A: Means “nothing but” or “only”; used with a negative verb.

---

Q: V-다음에
A: Means “after doing” or “next.”

---

Q: V-후에
A: Means “after doing.”

---

Q: V-(으)려고
A: Expresses intention or purpose; “in order to.”

---

Q: Adv-그리고
A: Connects ideas meaning “and.”

---

Q: Adv-그래서
A: Connects ideas meaning “so” or “therefore.”

---

Q: Adv-그렇지만
A: Means “however” or “but.”

---

Q: Adv-그런데
A: Introduces contrast or unexpected information.

---

Q: Adv-너무
A: Means “too” or “very,” depending on context.

---

Q: N-앞에
A: Means “in front of.”

---

Q: N-뒤에
A: Means “behind.”

---

Q: N-옆에
A: Means “next to.”

---

Q: N-위에
A: Means “on top of.”

---

Q: N-밑에
A: Means “under” or “beneath.”

---

Q: V-(으)세요
A: Polite imperative or honorific request.

---

Q: N/V-(으)로
A: Indicates direction, method, means, or choice.

---

Q: Adv-다
A: Means “all.”

---

Q: Adv-더
A: Means “more.”

---

Q: V-지 마세요
A: Polite negative command; “Don’t do…”
